Understanding Tracking Badges & Dues

The status of your bills is visually coded to help you quickly coordinate which dues require immediate action. The indicators adjust dynamically based on the current date:

Urgent: Less than 1 week (< 7 days)

Requires swift checkout to prevent delay or penalties.

Approaching: 8 to 14 days

Perfect time to reserve and secure necessary funds.

Distant: Over 14 days

Safely categorized for future attention in your cycle.

Settled / Income (Paid / Inbound)

Completed items or salary events cleared from attention.
